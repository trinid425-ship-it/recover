## Long description (main listing body)

Every subscription community on Whop quietly leaks money. A member's card
expires, a renewal fails, and their membership lapses — not because they wanted
to leave, but because a bank declined a charge. Across subscription businesses,
this kind of involuntary churn is 20–40% of all churn, and most of it is
completely preventable.

Recover is the always-on employee that gets that money back.

The moment a payment fails, Recover sends the member a friendly, one-tap link to
fix their card — then follows up on a proven schedule until it's resolved. When
someone cancels cleanly, it runs a softer win-back sequence with a reason to
return. And it watches your back on the risky stuff: chargebacks, refunds, and
members going quiet all surface as alerts before they cost you more.

You don't lift a finger. You just watch one number go up: **revenue saved this
month**, right on your dashboard inside Whop.

### What Recover does
- **Recovers failed payments.** Auto-DMs members a one-tap fix-your-card link the instant a charge fails, with smart follow-ups over 7 days.
- **Wins back cancellations.** A softer, offer-led sequence that brings cancelled members back.
- **Flags chargebacks & refunds.** Instant alerts so you can respond fast and protect your payouts.
- **Catches at-risk members.** Scores engagement and warns you before someone churns.
- **Shows your ROI.** A clean dashboard with recovered revenue, recovery rate, and win-backs in progress.

### Who it's for
Any creator running a paid community, course, or membership on Whop — trading
groups, coaching, content, or SaaS. If people pay you monthly, Recover pays for
itself.

### Why it's different
Most Whop apps add features. Recover adds revenue — the money you're already
earning but silently losing. It runs quietly in the background and proves its
value in dollars every month.
